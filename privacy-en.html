2. Local Processing of Photos and Media Data

The App is a local photo management tool designed to help you clean up duplicate photos and organize your photo timeline. To provide these features, the App requires the "Photos and videos" permission (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) to read the list of photos in your device's gallery.

All photo analysis (duplicate detection, similar photo grouping, timeline grouping, and local search) is performed entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded, backed up, or shared with any third party. The media permission you grant is used solely for displaying and managing photos within the App; you may revoke this permission at any time in your Android system settings. After revocation, the related features of the App will stop working, and no photo data will be retained.

3. Information Collection Statement (Core Statement)

The App integrates the Google AdMob advertising SDK to display in-app advertisements. The AdMob SDK collects the device's Advertising ID (AD-ID) as well as ad interaction data (such as ad impressions and clicks), which are used for ad delivery, ad personalization, ad performance measurement, and anti-fraud purposes.

You may disable the Advertising ID (AD-ID) at any time in your Android device's system settings. Once disabled, you will no longer receive personalized advertising based on that identifier, but you may still see non-personalized ads.

Apart from the Advertising ID and ad interaction data automatically obtained by the AdMob SDK, the App itself does not actively collect, store, record, transmit, or share any other personal or sensitive user data.

The App does not track user behavior. Specifically, the App will never access or process the following information:

  • Personal identity information (name, ID numbers, nicknames, personal profiles)
  • Device information (IMEI, Android ID, device serial numbers, MAC addresses)
  • Sensitive permission data (camera, microphone, location, contacts)
  • User usage logs, browsing history, or operation records
  • IP addresses, network information, or geolocation information

4. Third-Party SDK Disclosure

The App integrates the following third-party SDK: Google AdMob (advertising service).

Google AdMob is provided by Google, and its data collection and usage practices are governed by Google's own privacy policy. AdMob processes the Advertising ID and ad interaction data in accordance with its privacy policy for advertising-related purposes. Apart from the Google AdMob advertising SDK, the App does not integrate any other third-party analytics tools, advertising modules, data analysis tools, or social sharing plugins. Except for the AdMob advertising service, all other functionality of the App (including all photo analysis) runs entirely on the user's mobile device locally, with no additional external data interactions.

5. Data Storage and Data Retention

The collection, processing, and storage of the Advertising ID are handled by Google AdMob and are subject to Google's data retention rules. The App itself does not store the Advertising ID, nor does it store any other personal user data.

Your photo data is only processed temporarily in the device's memory. The App does not copy, upload, or retain any photo content. All of the App's configuration information is stored only in local cache on the user's device, and can be completely cleared via the system app settings or by uninstalling the App. The App does not perform any automatic data retention, backup, or synchronization.

The developer takes user privacy and data security seriously. All photo analysis is performed locally on the device. Data transmitted by AdMob is protected by Google according to its security standards. The App's code contains no malicious data collection, monitoring, or data leakage behavior.
