By installing and using this App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall and stop using the App immediately.

1. App Purpose & Local Photo Processing

This App is a local photo management tool. It requests the Photos and videos permission (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) to read your gallery photos for duplicate photo detection, similar photo grouping, timeline sorting and local search.

All photo analysis runs locally on your device. Your photos are never uploaded, sent to any server, backed up online or shared with any third party. You may revoke this media permission at any time in Android system settings. After revocation, photo-related features will stop working, and no photo data will be retained by the App.

2. Data Collection & AdMob Advertising

This App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. The AdMob SDK may automatically collect the following information for advertising purposes:

  • Android Advertising ID (AD-ID)
  • Ad interaction data, including ad impressions, clicks and ad performance signals

These data are used to serve ads, provide personalized ads, measure ad performance, and prevent ad fraud. All advertising-related data processing is handled by Google AdMob.

You can turn off personalized advertising by resetting or disabling the Advertising ID in your Android device settings. After disabling personalized ads, you may still receive non-personalized ads.

Except for data automatically collected by the AdMob SDK, this App itself does not collect, store or transmit any other personal information. The App does not collect IMEI, Android ID, MAC address, location, microphone, contacts, browsing history or logs.

3. Third-Party Service: Google AdMob

The only third-party SDK integrated in this App is Google AdMob (advertising service). AdMob is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. You can view Google’s privacy policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Data Storage

Photo content is only processed temporarily in device memory and is not saved externally. The App stores only local configuration data on your device cache, which can be cleared through App settings or uninstallation. The App does not sync or upload any user data to cloud servers.

Advertising ID and ad event data are processed and stored by Google, subject to Google’s own data retention rules.

5. Children’s Privacy

This App is intended for general audiences and is not targeted to children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us for deletion.

6. Your Privacy Controls

  • Revoke gallery permission in Android system settings to stop photo access;
  • Disable / reset Advertising ID to opt out of personalized ads;
  • Clear App cache or uninstall the App to remove local configuration data.
